Tom’s gbrain

This is a personal knowledge and memory system operated by Tom Lynch. It helps Tom search his information, recover past work, summarize activity, prepare writing, and reuse attributed templates through his own authorized AI clients.

The optional Google connection reads data only from accounts that Tom explicitly authorizes. It can read email, calendars, contacts, and Drive files, and uses the account’s verified email address to match each connection. These Google permissions do not allow this connection to send mail or change email, events, contacts, or files.

Information is indexed in Tom’s gbrain on his AWS server. Relevant information can be retrieved by Tom’s separately authorized AI clients for search, summaries, writing, and planning. The system’s data and account access are not publicly available through this page.

Access is restricted to Tom’s approved accounts and clients. This is not a public signup service.
