Privacy notice — Tom’s gbrain

Operator: Tom Lynch. For questions or deletion requests, contact Tom at lyncht248@gmail.com.

Information accessed

With account consent, the Google connection can read email and related metadata, calendar information and events, saved contacts, and accessible Drive file metadata and supported content. It also checks the account’s verified email address. The connection requests read-only Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, and Drive permissions, plus basic sign-in identity. It does not request permission to send mail or modify Google data.

Collection and indexing have format, size, history, and noise-filtering limits. A connected account does not mean that every file, attachment, revision, or historical message has been copied.

Purpose and AI access

Google information is used for Tom’s personal search, recall, summaries, writing, planning, and related memory features. Relevant retrieved information may be sent to the AI provider used by an AI client that Tom has separately authorized, to answer his request or perform his approved background processing. Each provider processes that information under the terms and settings of the account in use.

The brain also contains Tom’s separately captured activity summaries, conversation records, and authorized network information. Those sources retain their provenance. Public profile claims and other people’s writing are not treated as Tom’s own voice.

Storage and access

Imported content, derived memories, and search indexes are stored in Tom’s private system on his AWS server. Protected backups are kept on the server and Tom’s Mac. Access is controlled through approved accounts and source-specific permissions. The new Google connection credentials are stored only on the AWS server, separately from brain content, and are excluded from routine brain backups.

Disconnecting and deletion

Tom can revoke the app’s Google access through his Google Account permissions and stop the corresponding collector. Disconnecting an account does not automatically delete information already imported into the brain. Imported content, derived records, and backup copies require separate removal by Tom. Deleting a live record does not by itself erase older backup copies; these must be removed separately or expire through backup rotation. No fixed erasure deadline is promised by this notice.

Changes

If this system’s use of Google information changes, its disclosures must be updated before that new use begins, and any required account consent must be obtained.
